# Krosoft

Krosoft builds delivery software, internal tools, and Atlassian apps for teams that need clearer ownership and inspectable automation.

## Work

Krosoft focuses on practical systems that reduce coordination overhead, make operational work easier to inspect, and support reliable delivery.

Current public work includes:

- Atlassian apps for Jira and Confluence
- Internal tools for focused operational workflows
- Delivery and automation tooling
- Product architecture and service reliability work

## Approach

Krosoft prefers systems that make ownership, review, and operational boundaries explicit.

Important principles:

- Clear ownership over hidden responsibility
- Inspectable automation over opaque automation
- Human review for sensitive operational changes
- Governance rules enforced by systems where practical
- Tools that fit how work actually moves
